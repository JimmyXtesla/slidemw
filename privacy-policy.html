About this policy

This policy explains how SLIDE handles information when you use the SLIDE learning app and its related services. The app provides lessons, study planning, learning progress, notifications, and a student forum.

The app is presented as operated by SLIDE Educational Apps in Malawi. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@slideapp.mw.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile: name, email address, password (stored by the service as a password hash), school, class/form, and the city or district you provide.
  • Learning activity: lesson and study progress, scores, completed activities, streaks, experience points, saved flashcards, and study-plan or reminder settings.
  • Forum activity: posts, comments, likes, reports, and the display name associated with contributions.
  • Content you submit: text and images you choose to upload, including lesson or contribution images and cover images.
  • Notification information: your notification preferences and an app push token used to deliver notifications.
  • App and service data: authentication/session information and technical request data needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service. Hosting providers may process connection and diagnostic data.

We do not access your precise device location. If you provide a city or district in your profile, you enter or select that information yourself.

The app’s AI helper currently uses preset responses in the app; your prompts are not sent to an external AI provider by the current app implementation.

Device permissions

You can manage permissions in your device settings. A permission may be requested only when you use the related feature; denying it can limit that feature.

  • Photos and media library: lets you choose images for content contributions. The app uses the images you select; it does not need to scan your entire photo library.
  • Camera: camera access is declared in the app configuration, but the current app screens use the photo-library picker and do not capture photos with the camera. If camera capture is not added, this permission should be removed from the release build.
  • Microphone / audio recording: no current feature records audio. A dependency may add a microphone permission to some builds even though the app does not use it. We intend to remove this unused permission from the release configuration.
  • Notifications: allows the app to send study reminders and other app notifications. You can deny or later turn off notification permission in device settings; notification and reminder controls in the app may also be available.
  • Internet access: allows the app to connect to its service, retrieve learning content, submit contributions, and deliver forum and account features.

We do not request contacts, SMS, calendar, or GPS/location permission in the current app implementation.

Permission note: The camera and microphone disclosures above reflect permissions present or potentially added by the current build configuration, not active camera or audio features. The unused permissions should be removed before release. Store disclosures should be checked against the final Android and iOS build manifests.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide lessons and study tools, save progress, show forum activity, review reports, deliver notifications, process contributions, respond to support requests, and maintain the reliability and safety of the service.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising in the current app implementation. We did not find an advertising SDK in the app code reviewed for this policy.

Sharing and service providers

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the app:

  • SLIDE API and hosting: account, profile, progress, forum, notification, and contribution data are sent to the SLIDE backend hosted at xback-nine.vercel.app.
  • Cloudinary: images you submit as lesson or contribution content are uploaded to Cloudinary for image storage and delivery.
  • Expo and device push services: push tokens and notification payloads are processed to deliver push notifications. Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging may be involved depending on your device platform.

Forum posts, comments, and submitted learning content may be visible to other app users or moderators. Do not include private information in public contributions. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the service, or our rights.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and activity information while it is needed to provide the service, support learning features, address safety or abuse reports, and meet legal obligations. Public contributions may remain visible until removed by you where the app allows, or by a moderator or support team.

Current account deletion behavior: the in-app deletion flow removes the account record from the service. The current backend does not automatically delete every related record or uploaded item. Progress, forum posts or comments, contributions, reports, and some identifying content may therefore remain after the account record is removed. Email support@slideapp.mw to request removal of remaining information. We will review the request and explain what was removed or what must be retained.

Some limited information may be retained when needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. We do not currently publish fixed retention periods for each category of data.

Release requirement: account deletion must remove or anonymize associated personal data, including related learning records and user content, except data that must lawfully be retained. The deletion behavior described here should be improved and verified before public release.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including authenticated access to account features and server-side password hashing. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a unique password and keep your sign-in details private.

Children

SLIDE is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us at support@slideapp.mw so we can review and address it.

Your choices and rights

  • Update profile information in the app where profile editing is available.
  • Choose whether to grant photo-library or notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting support@slideapp.mw.
  • Request account deletion using the email link at the top of this page, even if you no longer have the app installed.

We may ask for information needed to verify that a request relates to your account. Do not include your password in a request.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice in the app when appropriate. Please check this page periodically.
